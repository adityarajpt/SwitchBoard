Plain-language privacy

Where do my files go? Here’s the simple answer.

SwitchBoard moves files between the phone, your Mac, and a browser page on your local network. Here is what that means for your files and your privacy.

When I use a USB cable, where do the files go?

When you save Android files to your Mac, the app asks where on the Mac to save them. It creates a copy there. The original stays on the phone. Uploading files to the phone sends a copy from your Mac to the connected phone.

Does SwitchBoard upload my files to a cloud?

No SwitchBoard cloud account or cloud file storage is part of this early-access build. A normal USB transfer happens between your Android phone and Mac.

What happens when I use Wi-Fi sharing?

The Mac starts a temporary webpage on your local Wi-Fi. You open it on the phone by scanning the QR code. The page lets you upload files to the Mac or download files you chose to share. Uploads are saved in Downloads → Transfer Hub.

The sharing page uses a local HTTP connection, not an encrypted HTTPS website. Someone with access to the temporary link on the same network may send files to the Mac or download the files you chose to share. Use it on Wi-Fi you trust and click Stop portal when done. The portal is not running until you start it.

Can I delete my phone's originals with SwitchBoard?

Saving files to the Mac copies them and leaves the originals on the phone. Folder sync also does not propagate deletions. Still, review what you choose to transfer and keep a separate backup of important files.

What does “not notarized” mean?

This early-access Mac app has not been notarized by Apple, so macOS may show a warning the first time it opens. That warning is about how the app was distributed; it is not proof that the app has been security-reviewed. Only download from the official SwitchBoard Releases page and read the installation warning before choosing whether to open it.
