# Send files between Android and Mac without a cable: an easy Wi-Fi guide

This beginner guide shows how to send photos and other files between an Android phone and a Mac using SwitchBoard's QR-code sharing page. You do not need a USB cable, Android settings changes, or a phone app. You do need both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

The app on the Mac is called **Transfer Hub**. It opens a temporary web page for your phone while sharing is turned on.

## Send photos from Android to Mac

1. Connect the Mac and Android phone to the same Wi-Fi network—for example, the same home Wi-Fi. If the phone is using cellular data instead, this will not work.
2. Open **Transfer Hub** on the Mac and click **Shared** on the left.
3. Click **Start sharing** (or **Set up sharing**). A QR code appears on the Mac.
4. Open the phone's Camera app and point it at the QR code. Tap the link that appears. If the camera does not recognize the code, use any QR-scanning option on the phone, or type the web address shown in Transfer Hub into the phone's browser.
5. On the page that opens on the phone, choose the photos or files to send. You can select more than one.
6. Wait for the page to finish sending. The files appear on the Mac in **Downloads → Transfer Hub**.
7. When finished, go back to Transfer Hub and click **Stop portal**.

The original photos stay on your phone. This makes a copy on the Mac.

## Send a file from Mac to Android

In Transfer Hub, open the connected phone's **Devices** page and click **Upload files**. Choose one or more files on the Mac. Then open or refresh the QR page on your phone and tap the file listed there to download it. The phone may ask where to save the download.

## If scanning the QR code does not work

- Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi name. Temporarily turn off cellular data on the phone while testing.
- Do not use a guest Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often keep phones and computers from seeing each other.
- If your phone or Mac has a VPN turned on, temporarily disconnect it and try again.
- If the Mac asks whether Transfer Hub may accept a network connection, allow it on your trusted home network.
- Stop sharing and start again to show a fresh QR code.

If you are using work, school, hotel, or public Wi-Fi, the network may block this kind of local connection. Try the [USB cable method](transfer-android-photos-to-mac-beginner.md) instead.

## Is Wi-Fi sharing private?

The transfer stays on your local network; SwitchBoard does not upload your files to a cloud account. While sharing is on, someone who can access the temporary link may send files to the Mac or download files you chose to share. Use a trusted private Wi-Fi network, share only files you intend to share, and click **Stop sharing** when finished.

## Questions

### Do I need to install SwitchBoard on Android?

No. The phone opens a web page in its browser. Transfer Hub runs on the Mac.

### Can I use my phone's mobile data and the Mac's Wi-Fi?

No. For the QR sharing page to connect, put both on the same Wi-Fi network.

### Can this directly sync everything between my Android phone and iPhone?

Not yet. The current Wi-Fi page lets a phone browser upload and download selected files. It does not automatically sync phone storage.
